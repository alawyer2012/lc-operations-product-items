Click-to-call outbound estimate

Reviewed Aug 27, 2026

Ryan asked for better data on outbound click-to-call volume for leasing offices — current estimates are a focal point for ELI Call Tracking plans, and he is not confident in them. Jacob pointed at Leasing Center recordings, with the requirement that inbound and outbound be split (Austin’s last pull was not). This page reviews the two costing workbooks against that ask, then costs the traffic on Entrata.

Working conclusion

The published CTC forecast is not measured traffic. Entrata Volumes (315k minutes in Q2 2026 → 6.52M in Q4 2027) rebuilds exactly from four hardcoded inputs: 10,000 units in June 2026, 12% month-over-month growth, 31.5 outbound minutes per unit per month, and 1 vanity number per 120-unit property. Those minutes come from 18 calls/day × 7 minutes × 30 days ÷ 120 units — not from LC CDRs or a cleaned PMC sample.

How the sheet number is built

Minutes per unit is the whole game. Adoption only scales it. The identity in Overall / Ryan C MASTER / Entrata Volumes is:

monthly minutes = units × 0.15 outbound calls/unit/day × 7 min × 30 days

That is 18 outbound calls per day on a 120-unit property. 18 × 7 × 30 / 120 = 31.5 min/unit/month. Vanity count = units / 120. Q2 2026 is only June (10,000 × 31.5 = 315,000). After that, units compound at 1.12× per month. The ramp matches Entrata Volumes to the integer.

Westover was diluted by ~3.8×, then averaged in

Mary Rose’s usable fact: 1/2/2024–6/28/2024, 20 properties, 77,610 calls, 50,978 outbound, 26,632 inbound, AHT under 3 minutes. Average Calls then divides outbound by 75 Salesforce properties and leaves inbound undivided at 4,439. That makes Westover look 97.5% inbound and 2.0 outbound min/unit. Repaired, it is 66% outbound and 5.9 min/unit — still nowhere near 31.5.

In vs out labels are swapped

Column L is titled “% Inbound” but computes outbound share. Column O is titled “% Outbound” but computes inbound share. The PMC average row happens to use the values correctly. The LC average row does not: it reports LC as 83% outbound and 17% inbound — the reverse of reality. Using undifferentiated LC or Call Tracking minutes as a CTC proxy is the failure Ryan called out. 2024 property Call Tracking is 19.3M calls at 2.24 min AHT — inbound ILS pattern, not office click-to-call.

Cost on Entrata — same adoption ramp, different intensity

Adoption is held fixed (10k units in Jun 2026, 12% MoM) so the comparison isolates traffic and rate. Vanity is 1 DID per property at Twilio current $0.575/mo (~$2.6k in 2027). Conversation minutes, not billed legs: Twilio CTC is two PSTN legs ($0.00495 × 2 = $0.0099). AWS is telephony + platform with a 12.5% EDS discount, not doubled.

Risks that will move the estimate

Product definition — PBX outbound is not click-to-call

CTC for ELI is an agent clicking a guest card. Average Calls is office-phone outbound: prospect follow-up, resident calls, vendors, transfers of inbound callers, and whatever “Courtesy Connection” and Funnel count as an outbound leg. Westover outbound being 66% of all office calls is a PBX mix, not a CRM-click mix. If only a fraction of outbound is prospect CTC, intensity falls again from the PMC figures — toward LC’s 0.30 min/unit, not toward 31.5.

Twilio Potential Mins labels the same series “Click To Call / Queue Calling (All Minutes).” Queue calling is a different architecture (often inbound + conference). If 31.5 was meant to cover both, the Entrata Volumes tab is mislabeled. If it was meant as CTC only, queue calling is missing.

Leasing Center and 2024 Call Tracking cannot be used raw

Jacob is right that LC has recordings and duration. Ryan is right that direction was missing last time. 2024 LC: 2.16M calls, 9.37M minutes, 4.33 min AHT. Property Call Tracking: 19.3M calls, 43.4M minutes, 2.24 min AHT (inbound ILS). The Twilio mins sheet then puts 9,365,748 LC minutes into each 2027 quarter after a half-quarter start — that is the 2024 annual total, reused as a quarterly run rate (~4×). Call Tracking migration is a separate (and currently broken) volume, not a CTC proof point.

LC is also 83% inbound. On-site CTC is outbound. Centralized agents will not match on-site leasing-office behavior. Use LC only as a direction-split, unit-normalized outbound series for agent-originated prospect calls.

Two-leg billing, SIP, and “Total Per Min” unit errors

Click-to-call usually places two calls: agent and prospect. The Twilio calculator correctly ×2s PSTN. Overall’s Twilio outbound rate of $0.0099 is already 2 × $0.00495. AWS is entered as one telephony minute plus platform and is not doubled. Overall then sums vanity $/month into “Total Per Min,” producing $0.807 for AWS and $0.585 for Twilio — those totals are not per-minute rates and should not be used in a cost model.

Architecture is a first-class volume question: a WebRTC/softphone leg to the agent replaces one PSTN leg. That is a 10–40% COGS swing before intensity moves.

Transcription is a bigger cost lever than DID count

Ryan C Summary prices inbound transcription at $0.20/hour ($0.0033/min) and keeps it off outbound. Ryan C MASTER marks Twilio transcription $0.027/min and applies it to outbound CTC, taking unit COGS from ~$0.32 to ~$1.17 and the 60% margin price from ~$0.79 to ~$2.92. Call Tracker+ is still shown at $0.75. If ELI Call Tracking plans include recording, summaries, and scoring on CTC, the $169k voice bill is the wrong conversation.

Adoption ramp is unanchored, and Twilio terms in the workbook are expired

10,000 units in June 2026 is ~83 properties. 12% MoM is ~39k units by June 2027 and ~77k by December 2027 (~641 properties). There is no seasonality, despite 2024 property calls peaking in Q3 at 1.43× Q4. There is no attach rate to existing Call Tracker (21,161 Veracity+Twilio numbers; 77,967 total tracking numbers).

TWILIO tab: draft sent 10/16/25, expired 11/15/25, estimated launch 12/1/25, $2M year-1 and $2.5M year-2 annual commitment on entire Twilio spend. Entrata’s existing Twilio bill in the sheet is SMS-heavy (Nov 2025 × 12 ≈ $1.09M SMS). Voice CTC at sheet scale is small relative to that commit; voice + the 4×-inflated Call Tracking migration figure is not. Entrata “Total Calls” is a dash — we do not have a current Entrata voice baseline. The calculator’s 1M CTC calls is a vendor placeholder, not this forecast.

What we need before this estimate is usable

Three independent numbers, then a rate card. Do not average them until each is direction-split and use-case-split.

1. Austin / LC CDRs

Monthly, 24+ months, property-level: direction, who originated (prospect / LC agent / on-site), whether the tracking DID was used as outbound CID, talk time vs billed time, answer vs attempt, IVR time excluded, unit count on that date. Filter to agent-originated prospect calls. If outbound CID is not the tracking number, LC will undercount on-site CTC — say so explicitly rather than treating silence as zero.

2. On-site office telephony, repaired

Finish AMLI, Hines, SimplyHome, Catalyst. For each property: units, business days, outbound attempts vs answered, and a split of outbound into prospect / resident / vendor / transfer. Confirm whether Cottonwood Courtesy Connection is guest-card click-to-call. Rebuild Westover on the 20-property extract (or the full residential list) without mixing denominators. Prefer medians over means; Cottonwood at 20.3 and Trinity at 3.45 should not be averaged into a fake “typical.”

3. Adoption, architecture, SKU

Named 10k-unit beachhead or replace it with a bookings funnel. Attach rate to existing Call Tracker vs net-new. Implementation properties/month. CTC vs queue vs softphone: how many PSTN legs, recording on or off, transcription on or off. DID policy (1 vs 5 vs 10). Which Twilio rates are actually in force in 2026. US-only vs Canada/international. Whether failed/short calls bill a full minute. Seasonality from 2024 Q3 peak should be in the quarterly shape even if the annual total is held.

Decisions before we re-forecast

  1. Define the traffic: guest-card CTC only, all on-site outbound, or CTC + queue calling?
  2. Until Austin’s split lands, treat 31.5 min/unit as a high case, not the base. Base should sit nearer 6–15 unless we prove Cottonwood-like CTC.
  3. Ask Austin for direction + originator + CID + duration at property-month grain. Do not accept another undifferentiated minute dump.
  4. Lock Westover: 20-property subset vs 75 residential vs 83 Salesforce rows.
  5. Lock architecture: 2 PSTN vs PSTN+SIP vs WebRTC. Cost AWS and Twilio on the same leg count.
  6. Lock ELI plan contents. If transcription/summaries ride on CTC minutes, they dwarf DID and possibly voice.
  7. Replace 12% MoM with a bookings/impl. capacity curve, or keep it only as an upside case. Add leasing seasonality.
  8. Confirm whether the Oct 2025 Twilio draft is live. Do not size a $2M commit off placeholder 1M CTC calls.

Recommendation

Do not take 16.8M minutes / $169k as the CTC cost on Entrata. Take it as the high, fully-ramped, long-call, 2-leg case. A repaired-PMC base on the same adoption ramp is about 6.0M minutes and ~$62k Twilio voice+DID in 2027. A Cottonwood-like case is ~10.9M / ~$110k. A transcription-included ELI SKU on the sheet ramp is ~$623k. The spread is the estimate. Closing it requires Austin’s inbound/outbound split and a one-line product definition of what “click to call” includes.
